## Full description
Kickvana is the football manager game where every decision counts, live.

MANAGE LIVE
When a real match kicks off, take over the dugout. Change shape, press higher or make a sub, and watch your alternate timeline split from what actually happened. Did you get it right? Share the card.

MANAGER CAREER
Build a squad of generated players, set tactics on a drag-and-drop board, win transfer battles, renew contracts, handle injuries and suspensions, and keep the board happy across full seasons. Every club, kit and badge is original.

LOWER LEAGUE MODE
A 24-club, League Two-style division with a 46-game season, automatic promotion and play-offs. Start at Leyton Marshes, our red-shirted East London club, or rename any club to match your own league. Share league packs with your friends.

CLUB HOUSE
• Weekly challenge: one famous moment, everyone gets the same squad. Scored 0–100 against what the model thinks was possible.
• Duels: draft from the same pool and budget as a friend; the engine settles it.
• Private leagues, streaks, daily objectives and achievements.
• Match alerts for kick-off, line-ups and full time.

PROPER MATCH VISUALS
A smooth 2D match with player names and numbers, a ball trail, heat maps, pass maps and an xG timeline.

FAIR BY DESIGN
No gambling, no odds, no loot boxes and no pay-to-win. Probabilities are for the game only.

Accessible: keyboard and screen-reader friendly, adjustable text size, reduced-motion option.

New? Play the 60-second tutorial match.

Kickvana is an independent game and is not affiliated with any league, club or data provider. Real club names appear only as factual text in live scores; there are no club crests.
