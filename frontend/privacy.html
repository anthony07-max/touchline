Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026.

Kickvana ("we") is run by Anthony Adusei-Poku, contactable at anthony00.aduseipoku@gmail.com. This policy explains what we collect when you use the Kickvana website and apps.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, a salted password hash, the date you confirmed you are 13 or over, and whether you verified your email.
  • Gameplay: careers, tactics, weekly-challenge entries, duels, leagues, share cards, achievements and streaks.
  • Notifications: if you turn on alerts, a push subscription endpoint (browser) or push token (app) and the fixtures you follow.
  • Technical logs: our host keeps standard server logs (IP address, user agent, request path) for security and debugging for a short period.

We don't use advertising trackers, analytics cookies or third-party ad networks, and we don't sell data.

Why (lawful basis)

To provide the game you asked for (contract), to keep the service secure (legitimate interests), and to send notifications you switched on (consent, which you can withdraw at any time).

Cookies and local storage

Kickvana uses no cookies for tracking. We store a few strictly necessary items in your browser's local storage (your sign-in token, your career save key and display preferences such as text size). Because they are essential for the service to work, no cookie banner is required; you can clear them any time in your browser settings.

Who processes it

Hosting: Render (servers in the EU/US). Email (when enabled): our SMTP provider. Push: your browser vendor's push service, or Apple/Google via Expo for the apps. Live football data is fetched by our servers from licensed providers; no personal data is sent to them.

How long

Until you delete your account. Inactive careers without an account may be removed after 12 months. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.

Your rights

You can access and download your data (Account → Export), delete your account and data instantly (Account → Delete), correct your details, object, or complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Email us for anything else.

Children

Kickvana is for people aged 13 and over. If you believe a younger child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will post updates here and highlight important changes in the app.
